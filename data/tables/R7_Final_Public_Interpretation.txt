
PROJECT FRAMING
---------------
Formal Public-Transport Accessibility and Spatial Service Gaps in Lagos, Nigeria

REFERENCE SCENARIO
------------------
Population-to-network connector: 500 m

STATEWIDE RESULTS
-----------------
Within 15 minutes : 2,557,743 people (22.02%)
Within 30 minutes : 6,206,326 people (53.44%)
Within 45 minutes : 8,350,756 people (71.90%)
Within 60 minutes : 9,232,088 people (79.49%)

Beyond 30 minutes or structural formal-network gap:
5,407,518 people (46.56%)

INTERPRETATION
--------------
The accessibility model represents walking access to OSM-mapped formal/core
bus, BRT and rail access points.

The 46.56% formal-access-gap result must NOT be interpreted as the
share of Lagos residents without transport.

Danfo, okada, keke and other informal/paratransit services are not
comprehensively represented in the retained formal/core transit dataset.

Connector sensitivity was evaluated at 250, 500, 750 and 1,000 m.
The 500 m scenario is retained as the reference for comparability.
