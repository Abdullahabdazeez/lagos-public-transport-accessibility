# Release Notes — v2.0.0

This release replaces the original Lagos accessibility interpretation with the validated 2026 reconstruction.

## Major changes

- Reframed the project as **formal/core public-transport accessibility** rather than general transport accessibility.
- Explicitly documented that danfo, okada, keke and other informal/paratransit systems are not comprehensively represented.
- Reconstructed the population-to-network connector and retained **500 m** as the reference scenario.
- Added connector sensitivity at **250 m, 750 m and 1,000 m**.
- Added **15-, 30-, 45- and 60-minute** accessibility results.
- Retained population outside the connector as a structural network gap instead of excluding it from the denominator.
- Separated structural walking-network gaps from time-based formal-transit service gaps.
- Reproduced the original 30-minute result while correcting its interpretation.

## Final statewide results

- ≤15 minutes: **2,557,743 people (22.02%)**
- ≤30 minutes: **6,206,326 people (53.44%)**
- ≤45 minutes: **8,350,756 people (71.90%)**
- ≤60 minutes: **9,232,088 people (79.49%)**
- Beyond 30 minutes or structural formal-network gap: **5,407,518 people (46.56%)**

The 46.56% value describes a gap relative to the mapped formal/core transit system. It does not mean that 46.56% of Lagos residents lack transport.

## Status

Analytical reconstruction complete. Final maps, report and public documentation validated. The scientific project board remains a separate visual-design task.
