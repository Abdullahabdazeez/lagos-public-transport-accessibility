# Limitations

- OpenStreetMap completeness varies spatially and changes over time.
- The model treats mapped access points as available services without measuring frequency, affordability, reliability or capacity.
- Walking speed is fixed at 4.8 km/h and does not represent age, disability, terrain, weather or pedestrian-environment differences.
- The 500 m connector threshold is an analytical assumption.
- WorldPop is a modelled population surface, not a household census.
- Water crossings and ferry connectivity are not represented in the core bus/rail accessibility measure.
- LGA averages can conceal neighbourhood-scale inequalities.
- The outputs support strategic screening and should not be treated as parcel-level transport design or investment approval.
