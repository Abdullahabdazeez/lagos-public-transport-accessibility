# Limitations

## Informal and paratransit mobility

The largest limitation is the representation of the transport system. The retained access-point dataset primarily represents mapped formal/core bus, Bus Rapid Transit and rail locations. Lagos mobility also depends heavily on informal and paratransit services, including danfo minibuses, okada motorcycles and keke tricycles. These systems are not comprehensively represented.

The results therefore measure accessibility to the **mapped formal/core transit network**, not total transport accessibility in Lagos. High service gaps in peripheral LGAs such as Badagry, Epe, Ibeju-Lekki and Ikorodu must not be interpreted as evidence that residents lack transport generally.

## Population-to-network connector

A 500 m population-to-network connector is retained as the reference scenario for comparability with the original project. Because this is a modelling assumption rather than a universal walking standard, 250 m, 750 m and 1,000 m alternatives were also tested.

Population beyond a connector threshold is retained in the denominator as a structural network gap rather than silently removed.

## Walking-network assumptions

OpenStreetMap completeness varies spatially and changes over time. Walking speed is fixed at 4.8 km/h and does not represent age, disability, pedestrian infrastructure, road-crossing conditions, weather, personal safety or other local walking constraints.

## Transit-service performance

The model measures geographic access to mapped transit points. It does not evaluate service frequency, waiting time, affordability, reliability, vehicle capacity, congestion, transfers or route quality.

## Population data

WorldPop is a modelled population surface rather than a household census. Results should be interpreted as spatial population estimates within the project framework.

## Geographic aggregation

LGA summaries can conceal neighbourhood-scale inequalities. The outputs support strategic screening and planning prioritisation rather than parcel-level route, station or investment design.
