# Lagos Public Transport Accessibility and Spatial Equity

This project assessed walking-time access to mapped core bus and rail points across Lagos State. It combined an OpenStreetMap pedestrian network with WorldPop 2020 population estimates and LGA boundaries.

Of 11.61 million people analysed, 53.44% were estimated to live within a 30-minute network walk, while 46.56% were underserved. The results show strong accessibility in the metropolitan core and major deficits in peripheral LGAs. Alimosho had the largest absolute underserved population, while Badagry, Epe, Ojo, Ibeju Lekki and Ikorodu formed the very-high-priority group.

The analysis provides a reproducible basis for strategic public-transport prioritisation, feeder-network assessment and spatial-equity monitoring.
